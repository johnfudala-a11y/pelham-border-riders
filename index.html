The club

Neighbors who build the trails

Every mile you ride in Pelham exists because a volunteer cleared it, signed it, bridged it or groomed it, and because a landowner said yes. The Border Riders are those volunteers.

Dedicated to connecting our community to safe and healthy outdoor activities.
